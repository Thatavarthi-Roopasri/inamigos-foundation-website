Our Initiatives

Driven by purpose and powered by people, our initiatives focus on education, empowerment, sustainability, animal welfare, and community development across India.

Project SEVA

Over 50,000+ meals and clothing distributed to underprivileged communities, creating meaningful social support and improving lives.

50,000+ Lives Supported

Project BACHPANSHALA

Bridging educational gaps by training underprivileged children through school education support, digital literacy and life skills development.

Education & Digital Literacy

Project JEEV

Dedicated to animal welfare by feeding 50+ stray animals daily through a strong volunteer network.

50+ Animals Fed Daily

Project UDAAN

Empowering women through collaboration with rural self-help groups while promoting financial independence, skill development and menstrual hygiene awareness.

Women Empowerment

Project PRAKRITI

Promoting sustainability and environmental conservation by planting 20,000+ saplings and supporting eco-friendly agricultural practices.

20,000+ Saplings Planted

Project VIKAS

Facilitating employment and skill development through internships and professional training. Trained 30,000+ interns in data operations, finance, research, content writing, digital marketing and social work.

30,000+ Interns Trained

At InAmigos Foundation, we believe in the power of collective action. Through transparency, partnerships, and community-driven efforts, we create measurable social impact and encourage individuals and organizations to join our mission.

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a registered non-profit organization founded to create lasting social impact by empowering communities through education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development across India.
