Who We Are

InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded on 23 September 2020. Our mission is to create lasting social impact by empowering communities through education, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, animal welfare, community development and skill development across India.

We believe in the power of collective action and work with volunteers, partners and communities to bring meaningful and lasting change.
